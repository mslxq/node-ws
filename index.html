Why Forests Matter — to Everyone, Everywhere

Forests cover less than one-third of Earth’s land, yet they support more than half of all known species. They stabilize climate, filter water, hold cultural memory, and provide livelihoods for communities worldwide.

Climate Stability

Trees absorb carbon, reduce extreme heat, and regulate global rainfall patterns essential to agriculture and food security.

Biodiversity

Rainforests, boreal forests, and cloud forests shelter millions of species — many found nowhere else on Earth.

Human Well‑Being

Over 1.6 billion people depend on forests for medicine, fuel, food, spiritual practices, and daily survival.

Global Threats to Forests

Deforestation, illegal logging, uncontrolled wildfires, pollution, and climate change are pushing some of the world’s most important ecosystems toward irreversible collapse.

  • Deforestation: Driven by agriculture, mining, and rapid development.
  • Climate Extremes: Mega‑droughts and heat waves increase forest mortality.
  • Wildlife Decline: Habitat loss threatens one million species with extinction.
  • Indigenous Rights: Many communities protecting forests face legal and physical dangers.

Protecting forests is not charity — it is survival.

Take Action — Wherever You Live

  1. Reduce: Choose certified sustainable wood and paper products.
  2. Support: Donate to or volunteer with verified forest protection NGOs.
  3. Restore: Participate in reforestation and native‑species restoration activities.
  4. Advocate: Encourage stronger climate and conservation policies.
  5. Educate: Share reliable information with your community and schools.
